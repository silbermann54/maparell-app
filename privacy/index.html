Health and fitness data are special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR. We process your data only to display your workouts and routes on a map, to compute the statistics shown in the app, and — if you allow it — to notify you about new workouts. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) and Art. 9 (2) (a) GDPR). We answer emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to your request (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The app itself makes no automated decisions within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR and creates no profiles; for advertising, see section 13.

All health, workout, and location data are stored exclusively on your device. The provider operates no servers and receives none of this data. We do not pass it to advertising partners or to any other third party, and we do not sell it. The only exceptions are the Apple system services described in section 12, which the app uses to show maps, place names, and search results. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

The data originate from Apple Health (HealthKit) on your device. The app can only read the data types you allow, and you can change this at any time. Your use of Apple Health and iCloud is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. Per Apple's rules, health data obtained via HealthKit are never used for advertising or shared with third parties for marketing.

7. Storage period

Data remain on your device until you delete individual workouts, delete the app, or withdraw Health access in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes the app's local database and the map pictures it has saved from your device. Workouts the app saved to Apple Health remain there until you delete them in Apple Health. Emails are deleted once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention periods apply.

You have the right to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), and data portability (Art. 20), and the right to object (Art. 21). You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU Member State of your residence or place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR). You can turn notifications off in the app under Account or in iOS Settings.
